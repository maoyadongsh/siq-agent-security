# External reproduction record

Please provide: exact repository SHA and corpus/verifier digests; track A/B/C; OS/architecture and tool versions; installation requirements; commands and every attempt; expected vs actual outcomes; verifier counts; deviations, time and costs; and optional independence statement. Do not infer independence from a different machine or generated account.

Attach only reviewed public reports, never complete daemon state, tokens, pairing codes or private model configuration. Use synthetic input replacements without breaking signed data. Confirm that you have the right to share your submitted material publicly under its declared license, and choose whether to be credited by name, account or anonymously. Reporting a bug does not require agreement to paper authorship or disclosure of private data. Vulnerabilities use SECURITY.md.

Maintainer checklist: reproduce or explain deviations; preserve failed attempts; verify digests and signatures with the stated verifier; record the contributor's actual consent and provenance; distinguish partial/full coverage; archive a correction without overwriting original evidence. Two external reproduction groups are a future milestone, not achieved by local automated reruns.
