Respectful collaboration around methods, evidence and reproducibility.

## Participation standards

Participate respectfully. Critique methods and evidence, explain reproducibility failures constructively, and welcome contributors across backgrounds and experience levels. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, publishing private information and persistent disruption are unacceptable in project Issues, PRs and Discussions.

## Reporting concerns

The current moderator is repository owner @maoyadongsh. For private conduct reports, use the repository's [private reporting entry](https://github.com/maoyadongsh/siq-agent-security/security/advisories/new), label the subject `Conduct report`, and omit unnecessary personal information. This administrative use does not mean the report is a software vulnerability. Public governance questions can use Discussions. Do not publish a complainant's identity or a private report without consent.

## Enforcement and appeals

The moderator may request clarification, warn participants, restrict a discussion or remove access according to severity and repeated behavior. Explain decisions to affected parties where privacy permits. Appeals can be submitted privately with new evidence. There is currently no independent moderator; a complaint about the sole maintainer requires an agreed independent reviewer before independent resolution can be claimed.
